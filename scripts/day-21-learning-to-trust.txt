
Peace be with you.
Welcome back to this Lenten journey —
a slow, honest walk toward the heart of God.
Wherever you are right now,
take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.

Today is Tuesday of the Third Week of Lent —
and today, God invites you into one of the most vulnerable,
most stretching,
and most beautiful movements of the spiritual life:

Trust.

Not the kind of trust that comes easily.
Not the kind that forms overnight.
Not the kind that feels effortless.

But the kind that grows slowly —
the kind your guide describes so clearly:

“Trust grows slowly, through small acts of surrender.”

Trust is not a switch you flip.
It’s a posture you learn.
A muscle you strengthen.
A relationship you grow into.

And Scripture gives us the invitation in simple words:
“Trust in the Lord with all your heart.”

All your heart —
not just the confident parts,
not just the parts that feel certain,
not just the parts that make sense.

Even the anxious parts.
Even the confused parts.
Even the parts that don’t know what comes next.

Trust is choosing God
when the path is unclear.
Trust is leaning on His wisdom
when your own understanding falls short.
Trust is believing He is guiding you
even when you can’t see the next step.

Maybe today you’re standing in a place
where the road ahead feels uncertain.
Maybe you’re waiting for clarity,
for direction,
for an answer that hasn’t come yet.

Maybe you’re carrying questions
that feel too heavy to hold alone.

And maybe —
in the quiet of your heart —
you’ve been asking God,
“Can I trust You with this?
With my future?
With my fears?
With my hopes?
With my life?”

Lent teaches you to say yes —
not all at once,
not perfectly,
but slowly,
honestly,
one surrender at a time.

Trust is not pretending you’re not afraid.
Trust is bringing your fear to God
and letting Him hold it with you.

Trust is not knowing the whole plan.
Trust is knowing the One who does.

Trust is not having control.
Trust is releasing control
into hands that are wiser,
gentler,
and more faithful than your own.

And here’s the quiet truth:
God has been guiding you
long before you realized it.
He has been leading your steps
even when you felt lost.
He has been faithful
even when you doubted.

Today, He simply invites you
to lean a little more.
To surrender a little deeper.
To trust Him with one more piece of your heart.

So let us pray:
God, teach me to trust You more deeply.
Lead me in Your wisdom
and guide my steps
even when I cannot see the way.
Amen.

Say slowly, three times today:
“Jesus, I trust in You.”  
Let the words become a breath,
a prayer,
a surrender.

Thank you for walking this day of Lent with me.
May trust take root in your heart
and guide your steps with peace.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
