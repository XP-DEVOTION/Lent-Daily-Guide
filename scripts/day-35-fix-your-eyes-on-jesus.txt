
Peace be with you.
Welcome back to this Lenten journey —
a slow, honest walk toward the heart of God.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.

Today is Tuesday of the Fifth Week of Lent,
and Scripture gives you a simple, powerful invitation:

“Fix your eyes on Jesus.”

Not on the problem.
Not on the fear.
Not on the uncertainty.
On Jesus.

Today’s reflection says:

“Endurance grows when your focus is on Christ. Lent strengthens your faith by teaching you to look beyond your struggles and fix your gaze on the One who leads you.”

Endurance is not about pushing harder.
It’s not about pretending you’re strong.
It’s not about forcing yourself through difficulty.

Endurance grows
when your gaze shifts
from what drains you
to the One who sustains you.

When Peter walked on water,
he didn’t sink because the waves were too strong —
he sank because he stopped looking at Jesus.

And the same is true for us.
When our eyes drift to the storm,
fear rises.
When our eyes drift to ourselves,
doubt grows.
But when our eyes return to Christ,
faith steadies.

Maybe today you’re carrying something heavy —
a responsibility,
a worry,
a question that won’t leave you alone.
Maybe you’ve been trying to endure
by sheer effort,
by holding everything together,
by pushing through on your own strength.

But endurance is not self‑reliance.
Endurance is surrender.
Endurance is trust.
Endurance is choosing, again and again,
to look at Jesus.

He is the One who leads you.
He is the One who strengthens you.
He is the One who walks with you
through every valley,
every challenge,
every moment that feels like too much.

Lent teaches you
to lift your eyes —
to look beyond the struggle
and toward the One
who has already overcome it.

Let today be a day
where your focus returns to Christ,
where your heart steadies,
and where your faith endures.


So let us pray:

Jesus, help me persevere.
Keep my eyes fixed on You.
Strengthen my heart
and steady my steps.
Amen.


Activity for today:  
Repeat throughout the day: “Jesus, I look to You.”

And sometime today —
maybe as you pause before a task,
maybe while you’re walking down a hallway,
maybe in a moment that feels small and quiet —
just whisper:

“Jesus, keep my eyes on You.”

Let that whisper become your offering.

Thank you for walking this day of Lent with me.
May your gaze stay fixed on Christ
and may your faith endure with strength and peace.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
