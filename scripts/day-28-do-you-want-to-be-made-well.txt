
Peace be with you.
Welcome back to this Lenten journey —
a slow, honest walk toward the heart of God.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath… let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.


Today is Tuesday of the Fourth Week of Lent —
and today, Jesus asks a question
that reaches deeper than it seems:

“Do you want to be made well?”

It’s a question He asks the man at the pool of Bethesda —
a man who has been stuck for years,
waiting for healing,
hoping for change,
but unable to move forward.

And Jesus meets him right there —
in the place of frustration,
in the place of longing,
in the place where hope has grown thin.

Today’s reflection says:

“Jesus meets you at the places where you feel stuck. His question is gentle but piercing: Do you desire healing? Lent invites you to open the places you hide, trusting that Christ’s touch restores what is wounded.”

Healing begins with honesty.
Not with strength.
Not with perfection.
Not with pretending everything is fine.

Healing begins when you let Jesus
into the places you’ve been avoiding —
the places that feel too tender,
too painful,
too complicated to face alone.

Maybe there’s a part of your heart
that feels stuck right now —
a habit you can’t break,
a wound you can’t forget,
a fear you can’t shake,
a heaviness you can’t name.

Jesus doesn’t force His way in.
He asks.
He invites.
He waits for your yes.

“Do you want to be made well?”
Not, “Are you strong enough?”
Not, “Have you earned it?”
Not, “Do you deserve it?”

Just —
“Do you want healing?”

Because healing is not about worthiness.
It’s about willingness.

Willingness to open the door.
Willingness to let grace touch what hurts.
Willingness to believe that renewal is possible
even in the places that feel beyond repair.

Jesus does not shame your wounds.
He restores them.
He does not rush your healing.
He walks with you through it.
He does not ask you to fix yourself.
He asks you to trust Him.

Let today be a day of courage —
a day where you bring one hidden place
into the light of Christ
and whisper,
“Lord, I desire healing.”

Because He is already standing beside you,
ready to make you whole.


So let us pray:

Lord, I desire healing.
Make me whole in Your love.
Touch what is wounded
and restore what has grown weary.
Amen.


Activity for today:  
At some point —
maybe in a quiet hallway,
maybe while you’re washing dishes,
maybe in the car,
or in a moment when no one else is watching —
just whisper:

“Lord, heal this place in me.”

Let that whisper become your offering.


Thank you for walking this day of Lent with me.
May Christ’s healing touch
renew your heart
and restore your spirit.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
