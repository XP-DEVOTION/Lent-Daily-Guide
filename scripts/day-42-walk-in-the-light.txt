
Peace be with you.
We continue our journey through Holy Week —
these sacred days where Jesus invites us
to walk closely beside Him.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.

Today is Tuesday of Holy Week,
and Jesus speaks a simple, urgent invitation:

“Walk while you have the light.”

Not later.
Not eventually.
Now.

Today’s reflection says:

“Jesus calls you to walk in the light—to live with honesty, courage, and faith. Holy Week is a time to let go of shadows and step fully into God’s truth.”

Walking in the light
is not about perfection —
it’s about direction.
It’s about choosing truth
over hiding,
courage over fear,
integrity over convenience.

Holy Week brings everything into the open.
It reveals the heart of Jesus —
a heart that is honest,
courageous,
faithful,
and full of love.

And it invites you
to let His light illuminate your own heart.

Maybe there is an area of your life
where shadows linger —
a conversation you’ve avoided,
a truth you’ve been afraid to face,
a habit you’ve kept tucked away,
a place where you’ve been walking carefully
instead of confidently.

Jesus does not expose you to shame you.
He shines His light to free you.

Light brings clarity.
Light brings healing.
Light brings courage.

And when you walk in the light,
you walk with Jesus —
step by step,
moment by moment,
heart to heart.

Let today be a day
where you choose honesty,
where you choose courage,
where you choose the light.

So let us pray:

Lord, help me walk in Your light
with integrity and courage.
Shine into every corner of my heart
and lead me in Your truth.
Amen.

Activity for today:  
Choose one area where you will walk in greater honesty today.

And sometime today —
maybe as you pause before speaking,
maybe while you’re stepping into a new moment,
maybe in a breath that feels clear and steady —
just whisper:

“Lord, let me walk in Your light.”

Let that whisper become your offering.

Thank you for walking this sacred day with me.
May the light of Christ guide your steps
and strengthen your heart
as Holy Week unfolds.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
