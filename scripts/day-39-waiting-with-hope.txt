
Peace be with you.
Welcome back to this Lenten journey —
a slow, honest walk toward the heart of God.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.

Today is Saturday of the Fifth Week of Lent,
and Scripture gives us a simple, honest prayer:

“I wait for the Lord,
my soul waits.”

Waiting is one of the hardest parts
of the spiritual life.
Not because waiting is empty,
but because waiting reveals
what we trust.

Today’s reflection says:

“Waiting is not passive—it is trust in motion. Lent teaches you to wait with hope, knowing that God is faithful and His promises are sure.”

Waiting with God
is not like waiting in line
or waiting for time to pass.
It is an active posture —
a leaning forward,
a steadying of the heart,
a quiet confidence
that God is working
even when you cannot see it.

Maybe today you find yourself waiting —
waiting for clarity,
waiting for healing,
waiting for direction,
waiting for a prayer to be answered.

Waiting can feel like stillness on the outside
and restlessness on the inside.
But Scripture reminds you
that waiting with God
is never wasted.

God is faithful.
God is present.
God is moving
in ways you cannot yet see.

Hope is not wishful thinking.
Hope is trust anchored in God’s character —
trust that His timing is wise,
His heart is good,
and His promises are sure.

Lent teaches you
to wait with expectation,
to wait with openness,
to wait with hope.

Because the God you are waiting for
is already near.

Let today be a day
where your waiting becomes prayer,
where your stillness becomes trust,
where your hope becomes steady.

So let us pray:

Lord, teach me to wait with hope.
Strengthen my trust
in Your timing
and in Your faithfulness.
Amen.


Activity for today:  
Offer one moment of waiting today as a prayer.

And sometime today —
maybe while you’re standing in line,
maybe while you’re waiting for something to load,
maybe in a pause you didn’t expect —
just whisper:

“Lord, I wait with You.”

Let that whisper become your offering.

Thank you for walking this day of Lent with me.
May your waiting be filled with hope
and your heart anchored in God’s faithfulness.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
