
Peace be with you.
Welcome back to this Lenten journey —
a slow, honest walk toward the heart of God.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.

Today is Friday of the Fifth Week of Lent,
and Jesus reveals something profound
about the nature of love:

Real love costs something.

Not because love demands pain,
but because love gives itself away.

Today’s reflection says:

“Love always costs something. Jesus shows the depth of His love by laying down His life. Lent invites you to contemplate this love that holds nothing back.”

The world often teaches us
that love is about what we receive —
affirmation, comfort, belonging.
But Jesus shows us
that the deepest love
is measured by what we give.

He is the Good Shepherd —
the One who sees your wounds,
your fears,
your wandering,
and chooses to lay down His life
so you may live.

Not reluctantly.
Not out of obligation.
But freely.
Willingly.
Completely.

Maybe today you’re aware
of the cost of loving someone —
the patience it requires,
the forgiveness it asks for,
the vulnerability it demands.

Maybe you’ve been loving quietly,
sacrificing in ways no one sees,
giving more than you feel you have.

Jesus understands that kind of love.
He lived it.
He embodies it.
He strengthens you to offer it.

And Lent invites you
to contemplate this love —
a love that holds nothing back,
a love that goes all the way to the cross,
a love that chooses you
again and again.

Let today be a day
where you sit with the mystery
of a God who loves you
at great cost —
and invites you to love
with generosity in return.


So let us pray:

Jesus, thank You
for Your sacrificial love.
Help me love with generosity,
with courage,
and with a heart shaped by Yours.
Amen.

Activity for today:  
Offer a small act of love for someone today.

And sometime today —
maybe as you pause before a task,
maybe while you’re walking from one room to another,
maybe in a moment that feels hidden and ordinary —
just whisper:

“Jesus, let this be love.”

Let that whisper become your offering.

Thank you for walking this day of Lent with me.
May the sacrificial love of Christ
shape your heart
and guide your steps.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
