
Peace be with you.
Welcome back to this Lenten journey —
a slow, honest walk toward the heart of God.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.

Today is Laetare Sunday —
the Fourth Sunday of Lent —
a day when the Church pauses
in the middle of the desert
to breathe,
to lift its eyes,
and to rejoice.

Not because the journey is over.
Not because everything is easy.
But because light is coming.

Today’s reflection says:

“Laetare Sunday is a pause of joy in the desert of Lent. God reminds you that the journey leads to light, not darkness. Christ shines into every shadow, bringing hope and renewal.”

This is the heart of today:
a reminder that Lent is not a season of despair,
but a season of becoming —
a season moving steadily toward resurrection.

Jesus says,
“I am the light of the world.”

Not a distant light.
Not a conditional light.
Not a light reserved for the worthy.

A light that shines
into every shadow of your life —
your doubts,
your fears,
your wounds,
your weariness.

A light that does not expose to shame,
but reveals to heal.
A light that does not blind,
but guides.
A light that does not flicker,
but remains steady
even when everything else feels uncertain.

Maybe you’ve been walking through a heavy week.
Maybe your heart feels tired.
Maybe you’ve been carrying something quietly
that no one else sees.

Laetare Sunday whispers:
There is light for you.
There is joy for you.
There is hope for you.

Not someday.
Not eventually.
But now —
in the middle of the journey.

Joy is not the absence of struggle.
Joy is the presence of Christ
in the midst of it.

So today, let your heart lift a little.
Let your spirit breathe.
Let the light of Christ
touch the places that feel dim or weary.

This is a day to remember
that Lent is leading you somewhere —
toward healing,
toward renewal,
toward resurrection.

And now…
So let us pray:

Jesus, light of the world,
shine in my heart.
Fill me with Your joy.
Let Your light guide me
and renew me from within.
Amen.

Activity for today:  
At some point —
maybe when you’re getting dressed,
maybe when you’re stepping outside,
maybe when you catch your reflection —
look at or wear something bright
as a reminder of God’s light
shining over your life.

And if you feel moved,
just whisper:

“Jesus, be my light.”

Thank you for walking this day of Lent with me.
May the light of Christ
fill your heart with joy
and guide your steps with hope.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
