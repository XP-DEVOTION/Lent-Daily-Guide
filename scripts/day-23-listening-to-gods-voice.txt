
Peace be with you.
Welcome back to this Lenten journey —
a slow, honest walk toward the heart of God.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.


Today is Thursday of the Third Week of Lent —
and today, Jesus invites you into something simple,
something quiet,
something deeply needed:

Listening.

Not just hearing.
Not just noticing.
But listening —
with intention,
with openness,
with a heart ready to receive.

Your guide for today says:

“God speaks in many ways—through Scripture, silence, people, and the movements of your heart.”

God is not silent.
But His voice is gentle.
It rarely shouts.
It rarely forces itself into your attention.
It comes like a whisper,
a nudge,
a stirring,
a quiet clarity that rises from within.

Jesus says,
“My sheep hear My voice.”  
Not because they are perfect.
Not because they are holy.
But because they stay close to the Shepherd.

Listening is an act of closeness.
It is the posture of a heart
that wants to be guided.

But we live in a world full of noise —
external noise,
internal noise,
the noise of expectations,
the noise of worry,
the noise of constant distraction.

Lent invites you to turn down the volume
so you can hear the One voice
that truly matters.

Maybe you’ve been longing for direction.
Maybe you’ve been asking God for clarity.
Maybe you’ve been praying for guidance
in a decision,
a relationship,
a season of uncertainty.

God is speaking —
but His voice is often found
in the quiet places.

In Scripture that suddenly feels alive.
In a moment of silence that softens your heart.
In the words of someone who loves you.
In the gentle tug of the Holy Spirit
nudging you toward peace.

Listening to God is not complicated.
It is simply making space.
It is choosing stillness
over hurry.
Presence
over distraction.
Attention
over noise.

And here’s the beautiful truth:
God’s voice always leads you toward love,
toward peace,
toward truth,
toward Himself.

If what you hear brings fear,
shame,
or confusion —
that is not the Shepherd.

His voice brings clarity.
His voice brings gentleness.
His voice brings life.

So today, let your heart be quiet enough
to hear the whisper of God.
Let your spirit be still enough
to notice His presence.
Let your ears be open enough
to recognize the Shepherd’s voice
calling you by name.

So let us pray: 
Jesus, help me hear Your voice today.
Quiet the noise within me
and open my heart
to Your gentle guidance.
Amen.


Spend one minute in silence —
no words,
no requests,
no agenda.
Just listen.


Thank you for walking this day of Lent with me.
May the Shepherd’s voice
guide your steps
and bring peace to your heart.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
