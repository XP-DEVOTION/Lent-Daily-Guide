Peace be with you.
Today is one of the most solemn and revealing days of Holy Week —
a day that invites you to look closely at the heart,
at loyalty,
at love,
at the choices that shape a soul.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into this sacred moment together.

This is Wednesday of Holy Week,
traditionally called Spy Wednesday —
the day Judas goes to the chief priests
and begins the path of betrayal.

A friend turns away.
A disciple steps into the shadows.
A heart that once walked with Jesus
chooses another road.

Today’s reflection says:

“Spy Wednesday reveals the contrast between betrayal and fidelity. As others turn away, Jesus invites you to stay close to Him. Holy Week calls you to remain faithful, even when the path is difficult.”

Spy Wednesday is not about pointing fingers at Judas —
it is about looking honestly at the places
where we, too, are tempted to turn away.

The moments when fear pulls us back.
The moments when comfort feels easier than courage.
The moments when silence feels safer than truth.
The moments when we choose distance
instead of devotion.

And yet —
in the middle of betrayal,
Jesus remains steady.
Faithful.
Present.

He does not withdraw His love.
He does not close His heart.
He does not stop inviting His disciples —
including you —
to stay close.

Holy Week is not just a story of what others did.
It is an invitation to choose
who you will be.

A person who stays near.
A person who remains faithful.
A person who keeps walking with Jesus
even when the path grows dark.

Maybe today you feel the pull of distraction,
or discouragement,
or spiritual fatigue.
Maybe you feel the temptation
to step back,
to disengage,
to drift.

Jesus sees that.
And He simply says:
Stay close.

Not perfectly.
Not flawlessly.
Just honestly.
Just wholeheartedly.
Just one step at a time.

Let today be a day
where you choose fidelity —
where you choose presence —
where you choose to remain
near the heart of Christ.

So let us pray:

Jesus, keep me close to You.
Strengthen my fidelity,
my devotion,
and my love.
Hold my heart steady
as I walk with You through Holy Week.
Amen.

Activity for today:  
Spend one quiet moment simply being with Jesus.

And sometime today —
maybe as you pause before a doorway,
maybe while you sit in a moment of stillness,
maybe in a breath that feels honest and unguarded —
just whisper:

“Jesus, I choose to stay close.”

Let that whisper become your offering.

Thank you for walking this sacred day with me.
May your heart remain close to Christ
as the mystery of Holy Week unfolds.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
