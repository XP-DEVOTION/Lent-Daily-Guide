
Peace be with you.
Welcome back to this Lenten journey —
a slow, honest walk toward the heart of God.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.

Today is Thursday of the Fifth Week of Lent,
and the Lord invites you into a posture
that prepares the soul for what is coming:

A heart that is humble.
A heart that is open.
A heart that is ready.

Today’s reflection says:

“As Lent nears its end, God invites you to prepare your heart for the mystery of the Passion. A humble heart is the soil where grace takes root.”

We are approaching Holy Week —
the most sacred days of the Christian year.
Days that reveal the depth of God’s love,
the cost of redemption,
and the beauty of a Savior
who gives everything for you.

But to receive what these days offer,
the heart must soften.
It must slow down.
It must become willing
to be shaped,
to be moved,
to be changed.

A broken and contrite heart
is not a heart God rejects —
it is a heart God draws near to.

Maybe there are places in your life
that feel unsettled,
unfinished,
or unready.
Maybe you feel distracted,
or tired,
or unsure how to enter
the mystery of the Passion
with depth and sincerity.

God is not asking for perfection.
He is asking for openness.

A prepared heart is not flawless —
it is honest.
It is willing to be seen.
It is willing to be held.
It is willing to be transformed.

As Lent draws toward its climax,
let your heart become soft soil —
a place where grace can take root,
grow,
and bear fruit.

Let today be a quiet turning,
a gentle opening,
a simple yes.


So let us pray:

Lord, prepare my heart for Holy Week.
Make me ready to walk with You
into the mystery of Your love.
Soften what is hardened,
steady what is restless,
and draw me close.
Amen.


Activity for today:  
Spend two minutes in silence, offering your heart to God.

And sometime today —
maybe as you pause before a doorway,
maybe while you sit for a moment in stillness,
maybe in a breath that feels unhurried —
just whisper:

“Lord, prepare my heart.”

Let that whisper become your offering.

Thank you for walking this day of Lent with me.
May your heart open gently
as you draw near to the mystery of Holy Week.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
