
Peace be with you.
We have entered Holy Week —
the sacred days where love is revealed
in its most costly, beautiful form.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.

Today is Monday of Holy Week,
and Scripture brings us into a moment
of pure, unfiltered devotion.

Mary kneels before Jesus.
She breaks open a jar of costly perfume.
She pours it out —
not sparingly,
not cautiously,
but completely.

Extravagant love.

Today’s reflection says:

“Mary’s act of anointing Jesus is a gesture of pure devotion. Holy Week invites you to offer Jesus your own costly love—your time, your attention, your heart.”

Mary’s act is shocking
because it is so wholehearted.
So vulnerable.
So generous.

She doesn’t calculate the cost.
She doesn’t worry about appearances.
She doesn’t hold anything back.

She gives Jesus
the most precious thing she has
because He is the most precious One she knows.

Holy Week invites you
into that same posture —
a love that is not measured,
not cautious,
not halfway.

A love that pours itself out.

Maybe there is something in your life
that feels costly to offer —
your time,
your attention,
your trust,
your surrender,
your heart.

Jesus does not demand it.
He receives it.
He honors it.
He treasures it.

Extravagant love is not about the size of the offering —
it is about the sincerity of the heart.

Mary’s devotion prepares Jesus for His burial.
Your devotion prepares your heart
for the mystery of the days ahead.

Let today be a day
where you offer Jesus something real,
something meaningful,
something that comes from the deepest part of you.


So let us pray:

Jesus, teach me
to love You with my whole heart.
Let my devotion be sincere,
generous,
and true.
Amen.

Activity for today:  
Offer Jesus one act of devotion today.

And sometime today —
maybe as you pause before a task,
maybe while you’re sitting quietly for a moment,
maybe in a breath that feels open and honest —
just whisper:

“Jesus, this is for You.”

Let that whisper become your offering.

Thank you for walking this sacred day with me.
May your heart open wide
to the extravagant love of Christ
as Holy Week unfolds.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
