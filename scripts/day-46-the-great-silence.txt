Peace be with you.
Today we arrive at the quietest day of the Christian year —
a day suspended between death and life,
between sorrow and joy,
between what has ended
and what has not yet begun.

Wherever you are, take a slow breath…
let your spirit settle…
and step gently into this sacred stillness.

Holy Saturday is the day of the great silence.

Jesus has been taken down from the cross.
His body has been wrapped in linen.
He has been laid in the tomb.
The stone has been rolled into place.

And the world…
goes quiet.

Today’s reflection says:

“Holy Saturday is a day of silence, waiting, and hidden hope. The world seems still, but God is at work in the depths. This day teaches you to trust God even when nothing seems to be happening—resurrection is near.”

Holy Saturday is the day
when nothing seems to move —
and yet everything is being transformed.

It is the day when God works
in the places we cannot see,
in the depths we cannot reach,
in the silence we cannot interpret.

Maybe there is a place in your life
that feels like Holy Saturday —
a place of waiting,
a place of uncertainty,
a place where prayers seem unanswered
and progress feels invisible.

But silence is not abandonment.
Stillness is not inactivity.
Waiting is not wasted.

In the tomb,
God is doing the deepest work —
the work of victory,
the work of renewal,
the work that prepares the world
for resurrection.

Holy Saturday teaches you
to trust God in the in‑between —
in the pauses,
in the unanswered questions,
in the quiet spaces where hope
is learning how to breathe again.

Let today be a day
where you rest in the mystery,
where you surrender the need to see,
and where you trust
that God is already moving
toward resurrection.


So let us pray:

Lord, teach me to wait with hope.
Prepare my heart
for the joy of resurrection.
Hold me in the quiet,
steady me in the silence,
and deepen my trust in You.
Amen.

Activity for today:  
Spend a few minutes in quiet reflection today.

And sometime today —
maybe as you sit in a still room,
maybe while you pause between tasks,
maybe in a breath that feels slow and gentle —
just whisper:

“Lord, I wait with You.”

Let that whisper become your offering.


Thank you for walking this 46‑day journey with me.
May the silence of Holy Saturday
fill your heart with peace,
with trust,
and with a hope that is ready
for the dawn of resurrection.

And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
