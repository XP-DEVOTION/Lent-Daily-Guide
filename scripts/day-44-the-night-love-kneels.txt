Peace be with you.
Tonight we enter the Sacred Triduum —
the holiest days of the Christian year.
These are the hours where love bends low,
where God kneels before humanity,
where the heart of Jesus is revealed
in humility, tenderness, and sacrifice.

Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into this holy night together.


Tonight is Holy Thursday,
the night of the Upper Room,
the night of the basin and the towel,
the night of the Eucharist,
the night love becomes service
and service becomes sacrifice.

Jesus looks at His disciples —
men who will misunderstand Him,
men who will scatter,
men who will deny Him —
and He kneels.

The King of the universe
washes their feet.

Today’s reflection says:

“Tonight begins the sacred Triduum. Jesus kneels to wash feet, showing that true greatness is found in humble love. He gives His Body and Blood as a gift of total self‑giving. Holy Thursday invites you to receive this love and to offer it to others.”

Holy Thursday is the night
when love becomes visible.
Not poetic.
Not symbolic.
Visible.

Jesus gives Himself completely —
in service,
in humility,
in bread and wine,
in a love that holds nothing back.

He says:

“Love one another
as I have loved you.”

Not as you feel like loving.
Not as the world teaches you to love.
As He loves —
with a love that kneels,
a love that serves,
a love that pours itself out
for the good of another.

Maybe tonight you feel the weight
of what love costs —
the patience it requires,
the forgiveness it asks for,
the vulnerability it demands.

Jesus understands that cost.
He embraced it.
He lived it.
He invites you into it.

Holy Thursday is not just a memory —
it is a call.
A call to humble love.
A call to generous love.
A call to self‑giving love.

Let tonight be a night
where your heart opens
to the love Jesus offers
and the love He asks you to share.


So let us pray:

Jesus, teach me
to love as You love.
Make my heart humble,
generous,
and ready to serve.
Amen.


Activity for today:  
Serve someone in a small, hidden way today.

And sometime tonight —
maybe as you pause before a task,
maybe while you’re alone for a moment,
maybe in a breath that feels quiet and sincere —
just whisper:

“Jesus, let my love look like Yours.”

Let that whisper become your offering.

Thank you for walking this sacred night with me.
May the humility of Christ
shape your heart,
and may His self‑giving love
guide your steps
as the Triduum unfolds.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
