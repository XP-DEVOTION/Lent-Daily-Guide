
Peace be with you.
Welcome back to this Lenten journey —
a slow, honest walk toward the heart of God.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.


Today is Friday of the Third Week of Lent —
and today, Jesus invites you into the very center
of His own heart:

Compassion.

Not pity.
Not sympathy from a distance.
But compassion —
the kind that sees,
the kind that feels,
the kind that moves toward another
with tenderness and dignity.

Your guide says it beautifully:

“Compassion is the heartbeat of Christ.”

This is who He is.
This is how He looks at you.
This is how He calls you to look at others.

Compassion is not weakness.
It is strength —
the strength to notice someone’s pain
instead of turning away.
The strength to see someone’s worth
when the world overlooks them.
The strength to respond with gentleness
in a world that often chooses hardness.

Lent invites you to see others
with the eyes of Christ —
to look beneath the surface,
beneath the behavior,
beneath the mask,
and recognize the sacredness
in every person you encounter.

Maybe today you’ll cross paths
with someone who is carrying more
than they can say out loud.
Someone who feels unseen.
Someone who feels alone.
Someone who needs a kindness
they don’t know how to ask for.

Compassion transforms them —
but it transforms you too.

Because when you choose compassion,
your heart becomes softer.
Your spirit becomes gentler.
Your life becomes more like Christ.

Compassion is not dramatic.
It’s not loud.
It’s not always noticed.

But it is holy.

It is the quiet work of love
moving through you
into the world.

And now…
So let us pray:

Lord, give me a compassionate heart.
Help me see others as You see them —
with tenderness,
with dignity,
with love.
Amen.


Activity for today:  
At some point —
maybe in a quiet hallway,
maybe while you’re washing dishes,
maybe in the car,
or in a moment when no one else is watching —
just whisper:

“Lord, make my heart compassionate.”

Let that whisper become your offering.


Thank you for walking this day of Lent with me.
May compassion shape your steps
and soften your heart
in every encounter today.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
