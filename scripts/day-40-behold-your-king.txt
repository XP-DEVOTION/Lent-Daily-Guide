
Peace be with you.
Today we enter Holy Week —
the most sacred days of our faith,
the doorway into the mystery of Christ’s Passion.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into this holy moment together.

Today is Palm Sunday,
the day Jesus enters Jerusalem
not with force,
not with spectacle,
but with humility.

A King on a donkey.
A Savior who chooses gentleness.
A Messiah who comes in peace.

The crowds cry out:

“Hosanna to the Son of David!”

They wave branches,
they lay their cloaks on the road,
they welcome Him with joy —
even if they do not yet understand
the kind of King He truly is.

Today’s reflection says:

“Palm Sunday begins the great mystery of Holy Week. Jesus enters Jerusalem not as a warrior but as a humble King. Lent now leads you to the threshold of the Passion, where love is revealed in its fullness.”

Palm Sunday is a paradox —
a celebration wrapped in sorrow,
a triumph that leads to a cross,
a King who conquers
not by taking life
but by giving His own.

Jesus enters Jerusalem
knowing what awaits Him —
the betrayal,
the suffering,
the weight of the world’s sin.

And still, He comes.
Still, He rides forward.
Still, He chooses love.

This is the King you follow —
a King who does not rule by fear,
but by self-giving love.
A King who does not demand your perfection,
but invites your heart.
A King who does not stay distant,
but draws near to your pain,
your questions,
your humanity.

Palm Sunday invites you
to behold your King —
to see Him clearly,
to welcome Him honestly,
to walk with Him
into the mystery of Holy Week.

Let today be a day
where your heart opens wide
to the love that holds nothing back.

So let us pray:

Jesus, King of my heart,
lead me into Holy Week
with reverence and love.
Prepare my spirit
to walk with You
through the mystery of Your Passion.
Amen.

Activity for today:  
Hold a small branch or leaf today and pray: “Hosanna in the highest.”

And sometime today —
maybe as you step outside,
maybe while you pause for a moment of stillness,
maybe in a breath that feels open and expectant —
just whisper:

“Jesus, I welcome You.”

Let that whisper become your offering.

Thank you for walking this sacred day with me.
May your heart be open
as you enter Holy Week,
and may you behold your King
with awe, love, and trust.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
