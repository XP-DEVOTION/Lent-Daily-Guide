
Peace be with you.
Welcome back to this Lenten journey —
a slow, honest walk toward the heart of God.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.


Today is Saturday of the Fourth Week of Lent,
and Jesus speaks a promise
that reaches into every place that feels tired,
worn,
or lifeless:

“I am the resurrection and the life.”

Not just at the end of time.
Not just in the distant future.
But here.
Now.
In the places where you feel stuck,
discouraged,
or unsure if anything can change.

Today’s reflection says:

“Jesus brings life where there seems to be none. Lent prepares your heart for resurrection—new beginnings, renewed hope, restored joy. Nothing is beyond God’s power to revive.”

There are parts of your life
that may feel like sealed tombs —
dreams that faded,
joy that dimmed,
hope that slipped through your fingers,
prayers that feel unanswered.

But resurrection is not just an event.
It is a Person.
And wherever Jesus is,
new life is possible.

He brings life
to what feels dead.
He brings hope
to what feels lost.
He brings renewal
to what feels worn down.

Lent is not a season of despair —
it is a season of preparation.
A season where God softens the soil of your heart
so something new can grow.
A season where He breathes life
into places you thought were finished.

Maybe there is an area of your life
that feels beyond repair —
a relationship,
a habit,
a dream,
a part of your heart
that has been quiet for too long.

Jesus does not fear those places.
He steps toward them.
He calls them by name.
He speaks life into them.

Nothing —
not your past,
not your mistakes,
not your fears —
is beyond His power to revive.

Let today be a day of hope.
A day where you dare to believe
that God is not done with you.
A day where you open your heart
to the possibility of resurrection.


So let us pray:

Lord, breathe new life
into what feels dead within me.
Renew my hope,
restore my joy,
and awaken my heart.
Amen.

Activity for today:  
Reflect on one area where you desire new life.

And sometime today —
maybe as you pause between tasks,
maybe while you’re stepping outside,
maybe in a moment that feels small and quiet —
just whisper:

“Lord, bring new life here.”

Let that whisper become your offering.

Thank you for walking this day of Lent with me.
May the promise of resurrection
fill your heart with hope
and open your life to new beginnings.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
