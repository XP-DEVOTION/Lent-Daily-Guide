
Peace be with you.
Welcome back to this Lenten journey —
a slow, honest walk toward the heart of God.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.

Today is Wednesday of the Fifth Week of Lent,
and God speaks a promise
that reaches into the deepest places of loneliness and longing:

“I have engraved you on the palms of My hands.”

Not written lightly.
Not sketched in pencil.
Engraved —
held permanently,
intimately,
unforgettably.

Today’s reflection says:

“God never forgets you. Even when you feel unseen or overlooked, He holds you close. Lent reminds you that you are always in God’s heart and always in His hands.”

There are moments in life
when you feel unnoticed —
when your efforts go unseen,
when your prayers feel unheard,
when your presence feels small
in a world that moves quickly.

But God does not overlook you.
He does not forget you.
He does not lose track of your heart.

You are engraved on His hands —
carried in His memory,
held in His love,
seen in every moment of your life.

Maybe today you feel a little unseen —
by people,
by circumstances,
by the world around you.
Maybe you’ve been carrying something quietly,
hoping someone would notice,
hoping someone would care.

God notices.
God cares.
God remembers.

His faithfulness is not fragile.
It does not fade with time.
It does not weaken with distance.
It does not depend on your perfection.

He remembers you
because you belong to Him.

And Lent is the season
that brings you back to this truth —
that you are held,
you are known,
you are loved,
you are remembered.

Let today be a day
where you rest in God’s faithfulness,
where you trust that He sees you fully
and holds you completely.


So let us pray:

Lord, thank You for remembering me.
Help me trust Your faithfulness.
Hold my heart close
and steady my steps in Your love.
Amen.

Activity for today:  
Write down one way God has been faithful to you.

And sometime today —
maybe as you pause before a task,
maybe while you’re sitting quietly for a moment,
maybe in a breath that feels still and gentle —
just whisper:

“Lord, thank You for remembering me.”

Let that whisper become your offering.

Thank you for walking this day of Lent with me.
May God’s faithfulness steady your heart
and remind you that you are never forgotten.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
