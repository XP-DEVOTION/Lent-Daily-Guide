
Peace be with you.
Welcome back to this Lenten journey —
a slow, honest walk toward the heart of God.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.


Today is Wednesday of the Third Week of Lent —
and today, Jesus brings us back to the center of everything.
Not rules.
Not rituals.
Not complexity.

Love.

Your guide says it simply and beautifully:

“Holiness is not complicated—it is love lived out in daily life.”

Jesus is asked a question meant to trap Him:
“What is the greatest commandment?”
And He answers with clarity that cuts through centuries of debate:

“Love God with all your heart…
and love your neighbor as yourself.”

This is the heart of holiness.
This is the core of the Gospel.
This is the life God invites you into.

But love — real love —
is not always easy.

Loving God with all your heart
means letting Him into every part of your life:
your hopes,
your fears,
your wounds,
your desires,
your questions.

It means trusting Him enough
to let Him shape you from the inside out.

And loving your neighbor as yourself —
that’s where love becomes real.
Not in theory.
Not in sentiment.
But in the small, hidden, everyday choices
that reveal who you truly are.

Love looks like patience
when you feel rushed.
Compassion
when someone is hurting.
Kindness
when it costs you something.
Forgiveness
when it feels undeserved.
Attention
when someone needs to be seen.

Holiness is not perfection.
Holiness is love.

Love lived in the ordinary.
Love lived in the unnoticed.
Love lived in the places
where no one applauds
but God sees.

Lent calls you back to this simplicity —
to the truth that spiritual maturity
is not measured by how much you know,
but by how deeply you love.

Maybe today God is inviting you
to love someone who is difficult.
Or someone who is hurting.
Or someone who feels forgotten.
Or maybe…
He’s inviting you to love yourself
with the same gentleness
He shows you.

Wherever love is needed today,
that is where holiness begins.

So let us pray: 
Lord, increase my love for You
and for those around me.
Make my heart like Yours.
Amen.


Do one small act of kindness today —
something simple,
something quiet,
something done in love.


Thank you for walking this day of Lent with me.
May love guide your steps
and shape your heart
in every moment of today.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
