
Peace be with you.
Welcome back to this Lenten journey —
a slow, honest walk toward the heart of God.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.

Today is Saturday of the Third Week of Lent —
and today, God speaks a word
that can feel intimidating at first,
but is actually one of the most beautiful invitations
in all of Scripture:

“Be holy, for I am holy.”

Holiness is not perfection.
Holiness is not flawlessness.
Holiness is not living without mistakes.

Today’s reflection says:

“Holiness is not perfection — it is belonging to God. Lent reminds you that you are set apart for love, mercy, and goodness. God calls you to reflect His heart in the world.”

Holiness is belonging.
Holiness is relationship.
Holiness is letting God shape your life
so that His love becomes visible through you.

Holiness is not about being impressive.
It’s about being available.
Available to God’s love.
Available to God’s shaping.
Available to God’s presence
in the ordinary moments of your day.

Maybe you’ve thought holiness was for other people —
for saints,
for mystics,
for people who seem to have everything together.

But holiness is for you.
Right where you are.
In your real life.
In your real struggles.
In your real imperfections.

Holiness is God’s work in you —
and your willingness to say yes.

Holiness looks like patience
when you want to rush.
Gentleness
when you want to react.
Mercy
when you want to judge.
Kindness
when you want to withdraw.

Holiness is not a performance.
It’s a posture.
A way of standing before God
with an open heart
and a willing spirit.

And Lent is the season
that reminds you who you belong to —
and who you are becoming.

You are set apart for love.
Set apart for goodness.
Set apart for God.

And now…
So let us pray:

God, make me holy as You are holy.
Shape my life according to Your love.
Let my heart reflect Your goodness
in everything I do.
Amen.

Activity for today:  
At some point —
maybe in a quiet hallway,
maybe while you’re washing dishes,
maybe in the car,
or in a moment when no one else is watching —
just whisper:

“Lord, make me holy.”

Let that whisper become your offering.

Thank you for walking this day of Lent with me.
May holiness take root in your heart
and shine gently through your life.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
