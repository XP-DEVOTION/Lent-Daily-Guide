
Peace be with you.
Welcome back to this Lenten journey —
a slow, honest walk toward the heart of God.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath…
let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.

Today is Friday of the Fourth Week of Lent,
and Jesus speaks a call that is both challenging
and deeply beautiful:

“Take up your cross daily and follow Me.”

Not once.
Not occasionally.
Daily.

Today’s reflection says:

“Sacrifice is not about suffering for its own sake—it is about love. Jesus invites you to carry your cross with Him, trusting that every sacrifice offered in love bears fruit.”

Sacrifice is one of the most misunderstood words
in the spiritual life.
It is not about punishment.
It is not about proving your worth.
It is not about earning God’s love.

Sacrifice is about joining your heart
to the heart of Christ.

It is about choosing love
when selfishness would be easier.
Choosing generosity
when comfort calls louder.
Choosing patience
when frustration rises.
Choosing forgiveness
when resentment feels justified.

Sacrifice is love that costs something —
and because it costs something,
it becomes holy.

Jesus does not ask you
to carry your cross alone.
He carries it with you.
He carries it before you.
He carries it within you.

Maybe today there is a small cross
you’ve been avoiding —
a conversation you don’t want to have,
a responsibility that feels heavy,
a person who is difficult to love,
a habit you’re trying to surrender.

Jesus doesn’t shame you for hesitating.
He simply invites you to walk with Him.
To trust that every sacrifice offered in love
bears fruit you may not see yet —
fruit in your heart,
fruit in your relationships,
fruit in the world.

Sacrifice is not about losing.
It is about becoming.
Becoming more like Christ.
Becoming more open.
Becoming more free.

Let today be a day
where you offer something small
with great love.


So let us pray:

Jesus, teach me to offer my sacrifices
with love and humility.
Help me carry my cross with You
and let every offering
bear fruit in Your love.
Amen.


Activity for today:  
Offer a small sacrifice today for someone in need.

And sometime today —
maybe as you pause before a task,
maybe while you’re walking from one room to another,
maybe in a moment that feels hidden and ordinary —
just whisper:

“Jesus, let this be for love.”

Let that whisper become your offering.

Thank you for walking this day of Lent with me.
May every sacrifice you offer today
draw you closer to the heart of Christ.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
