
Peace be with you.
Welcome back to this Lenten journey — a slow, honest walk toward the heart of God.
Wherever you are right now, take a breath… let your spirit settle…
and let’s step into today together.

Today is Wednesday of the Fourth Week of Lent,
and Jesus speaks a word that reaches into every shadowed place of the heart:

“I am the light of the world.”

Today’s reflection says:

“Even in seasons of darkness, Christ shines. His light does not shame you—it guides you. Lent teaches you to walk toward the light, trusting that no darkness is too deep for His love.”

There are seasons when darkness feels close —
when confusion clouds your mind,
when grief sits heavy on your chest,
when fear whispers louder than hope.

But darkness is never the end of the story.

Christ steps into the shadows
not to expose you,
not to embarrass you,
but to guide you.

His light is gentle.
His light is patient.
His light is steady.

Maybe there’s a place in your life right now
that feels dim or uncertain.
A place where you’re not sure what comes next.
A place where you’ve been walking carefully,
hoping for clarity,
hoping for peace.

Jesus meets you there.
He doesn’t ask you to run toward the light —
He simply asks you to take one step.

Because no darkness is too deep
for His love to reach.

So let us pray:

Jesus, be my light.
Guide my steps and brighten my heart.
Shine into every shadow
and lead me toward hope.
Amen.

Activity for today:  
Light a candle today and pray for Christ’s light in your life.

And at some point —
maybe in an elevator,
maybe before you open a door,
maybe right before you speak —
just whisper:

“Jesus, shine Your light here.”

Let that whisper become your offering.

Thank you for walking this day of Lent with me.
May the light of Christ guide your steps
and brighten every corner of your heart.
And until we meet again —
peace be with you.
